# Rietveld Schröder House

The Rietveld Schröder House, designed by Gerrit Rietveld in 1924, is a masterpiece of the De Stijl movement. Its flexible interior spaces and pioneering use of rectangular forms and primary colours exemplify modernist architecture.

Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2004, the house represents both a built manifesto of the De Stijl principles and a residential experiment in open, transformable space.
