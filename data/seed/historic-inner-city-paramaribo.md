# Historic Inner City of Paramaribo

The historic inner city of Paramaribo has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2002. Founded in the 17th century as a Dutch colonial trading post on the Suriname River, its street plan remains intact and features wooden buildings that illustrate the gradual fusion of Dutch and local architectural traditions.

The site includes the Presidential Palace, Fort Zeelandia, and several historic churches and synagogues, all reflecting the multicultural heritage of Suriname.
